# 1. Business Understanding

## 1.1 Business Problem

The UK housing market has experienced substantial changes in house prices, regional performance, and transaction behaviour over the past two decades. However, these trends vary considerably depending on geographical location, property type, and buyer characteristics.

This project aims to analyse UK House Price Index data to understand how the housing market has evolved over time and identify significant differences between regions, property types, and buyer segments.

## 1.2 Project Objective

The primary objective is to use data analysis to identify long-term housing-market trends, regional differences, property-type patterns, and changes in transaction behaviour, and to translate these findings into meaningful business insights.

## 1.3 Business Objectives

The analysis focuses on five key objectives:

1. Analyse long-term changes in UK house prices.
2. Compare house-price levels and growth across UK regions.
3. Analyse differences in prices and growth between property types.
4. Investigate buyer and transaction characteristics, including cash and mortgage purchases and first-time buyers.
5. Identify significant market trends, opportunities, and potential risks.

## 1.4 Key Business Questions

- How has the UK housing market changed over time?
- Which regions have the highest house prices?
- Which regions have experienced the strongest long-term growth?
- How do different property types compare in terms of price and growth?
- How do housing trends differ between regions?
- How do first-time buyers compare with former owner-occupiers?
- How do cash and mortgage transactions differ?
- How do new-build properties compare with existing properties?
- Which regions show particularly strong or weak market performance?
- Which periods experienced significant changes in the housing market?

## 1.5 Potential Stakeholders

The findings could be useful to estate agents, property investors, property developers, mortgage and financial organisations, government and local authorities, housing researchers, and individuals interested in understanding regional housing markets.

## 1.6 Expected Deliverables

The project produces:

- A cleaned and documented dataset
- A data-quality assessment
- Exploratory analysis using Python/Pandas
- SQL-based business analysis
- An interactive Power BI dashboard
- Key findings and visualisations
- Business recommendations based on the analytical results

## 1.7 Analytical Approach

The project follows the data analytics lifecycle:

**Business Understanding → Data Understanding → Data Cleaning → Exploratory Data Analysis → SQL Analysis → Dashboard Development → Insights & Recommendations**
